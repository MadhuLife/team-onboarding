## How To Access a Repo on GitHub (Browser)

1. Go to [https://github.com](https://github.com) and sign in
2. Click your profile icon in the top-right
3. Click **Your repositories** — you will NOT see these here because they belong to MadhuLife
4. Instead, go directly to the URL (e.g. https://github.com/MadhuLife/company-docs)
5. You can also find them under the **Organizations** section of your GitHub profile

## How To Find Files Quickly in Obsidian

Once you have a vault open in Obsidian:

- Press `Ctrl + O` (Windows) or `Cmd + O` (Mac) to open the Quick Switcher
- Type any part of the file name and press **Enter** to open it
- This is the fastest way to jump directly to any document

---

## Staying Up To Date

Repositories are updated regularly. To make sure you always have the latest files:

1. Open **GitHub Desktop**
2. Select the repository you want to update
3. Click **Fetch origin** — if updates are available, click **Pull origin**

Do this at the start of each day before opening files in Obsidian.
