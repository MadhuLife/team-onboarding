# Step 1 — Create Your GitHub Account

## What Is GitHub?

GitHub is where we store everything: strategy documents, SOPs, templates, code, and project files. Think of it like Google Drive, but built for teams and version-controlled so nothing ever gets lost or overwritten by accident.

Every person on the team needs a free GitHub account to access our shared repositories (folders).

---

## Create Your Account

1. Open your web browser and go to [https://github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup)
2. Enter your email address and click **Continue**
3. Create a password — make it strong and save it somewhere safe
4. Choose a username:
   - Use something professional (your name or initials works fine)
   - This is what you will share with Dr-mkelvo
   - Example: `jane-smith` or `jsmith-team`
5. Click **Continue**
6. GitHub will ask whether you want to receive product updates — choose **No** or **Yes**, it does not matter
7. Solve the CAPTCHA puzzle if prompted
8. Click **Create account**

---

## Verify Your Email

1. Check your inbox for an email from GitHub with the subject **"Please verify your email address"**
2. Open that email and click **Verify email address**
3. If you do not see the email within 2 minutes, check your spam or junk folder
4. After clicking the link, your browser will confirm that your email is verified

---

## Set Up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

GitHub will prompt you to secure your account with 2FA. This is required. It adds a second step when logging in so only you can access your account.

1. After verifying your email, GitHub will walk you through the 2FA setup
2. Choose **Authenticator app** — this is the most reliable option
3. If you do not have an authenticator app:
   - **On iPhone:** Download **Google Authenticator** from the App Store
   - **On Android:** Download **Google Authenticator** from the Play Store
4. Open the authenticator app, tap the **+** icon, and scan the QR code shown on GitHub
5. Type the 6-digit code from the app into GitHub and click **Continue**
6. GitHub will give you **recovery codes** — save these in a safe place (a notes app, printed out, or password manager). These let you get back in if you lose your phone.
7. Click **I have saved my recovery codes**

---

## Send Your Username to Dr-mkelvo

Once your account is created and your email is verified:

1. Copy your GitHub username (visible in the top-right corner of GitHub when you are logged in, or at `github.com/your-username`)
2. Send the username to Dr-mkelvo via the team communication channel

Dr-mkelvo will then send you an invitation to each of the team repositories.

---

## Accept Your Repository Invitations

After Dr-mkelvo adds you:

1. You will receive one email per repository with the subject **"You have been invited to collaborate on a repository"**
2. Open each email and click **View Invitation**
3. On the GitHub page that opens, click **Accept invitation**
4. Repeat for each repository email

You will receive invitations to the following repositories:

- **madhu-life** — core strategy and project documentation
- **company-docs** — SOPs, PDFs, and business reference documents
- **ai-video-organizer** — video content and AI tools
- **claude-for-coaches** — coaching templates, scripts, and tools
- **leads** — lead research data and outreach tools

Once all invitations are accepted, move to Step 2.

---

## Already Have a GitHub Account?

If you already have one:

1. Log in at [https://github.com](https://github.com)
2. Confirm your email is verified (check Settings → Emails)
3. Confirm 2FA is enabled (check Settings → Password and authentication)
4. Send your username to Dr-mkelvo and wait for your invitations
